# A tag vocabulary. A member's tag is its family followed by its own title:
# the level-1 headline is the family and each child's title continues the
# name, so the child `-1_should` of the family `priority` is the tag
# `priority-1_should`, and `-26-01-a` under `trips` is `trips-26-01-a`. A
# deeper child continues its parent's name. A hyphen always starts a new
# segment; an underscore is part of one, so the level-1 headline `zz-inbox`
# is the member `-inbox` of the (unwritten) family `zz`.
